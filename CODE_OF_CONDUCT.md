# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume good faith, discuss the work rather than the person, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct puts people or the project at risk. Report conduct concerns through a private channel provided by the maintainers. If no such channel is available, open a short issue requesting private follow-up without naming people or posting evidence publicly. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.
